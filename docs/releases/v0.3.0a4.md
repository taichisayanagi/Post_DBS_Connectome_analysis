# v0.3.0a4 — small synthetic integration demo

- New input-free GUI/CLI demo with actual dcm2niix and MRtrix execution.
- Artificial CT/T1 DICOM and diffusion NIfTI → CSD → CT/MRI candidate mask →
  ACT/iFOD2 → strict electrode exclusion → SIFT2 refit → connectome → G1–G4.
- Independent DICOM value/geometry, mask coverage, segment exclusion, SIFT2 weight
  and endpoint-to-matrix checks. Graph/node/edge exports and a local result report.
- No patient inputs accepted; no clinical QC approval generated.
- Known synthetic atlas/5TT substitute for expensive segmentation. The demo does not
  establish full raw-patient-image-to-paper reproduction or clinical validity.

Requires separately installed MRtrix3 and dcm2niix for this demo. Other operations
retain their own external dependencies. No external binaries, atlas resources or patient
data are bundled. MIT for new code; third-party licenses remain separate.
